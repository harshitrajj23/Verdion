# Phase 5C — Evidence Provenance, Passage Traceability, and Analysis Integrity

## Overview

In regulatory decision-making, an AI model summarizing a legal opinion is untrustworthy unless every factual and statutory proposition can be directly traced back to a specific, immutable document version and character-bounded passage.

Option C enforces **deterministic source-to-claim traceability** across EUR-Lex, CJEU/CURIA, and EDPB sources.

---

## 1. Evidence Source Provenance

Every ingested source document is registered with:
- **Originating Publisher:** e.g., European Parliament and Council, Court of Justice of the European Union, European Data Protection Board.
- **Official Document Identifier:** Stable regulatory identifier, such as:
  - CELEX number (e.g., `32016R0679` for GDPR, `32024R1689` for EU AI Act)
  - ECLI identifier (e.g., `ECLI:EU:C:2020:559` for Case C-311/18 Schrems II)
  - EDPB Guideline number (e.g., `Guidelines 05/2021 on the Interplay between Article 3 and Chapter V`)
- **Source URL:** Direct canonical URL in the publisher's official portal.
- **Retrieval Timestamp:** Exact UTC ISO 8601 timestamp when fetched.
- **Document Version:** Publication date or official consolidated version date.
- **Normalization Procedure:** Standardized pipeline (`v1.0-clean_whitespace`) eliminating carriage returns and multiple consecutive spaces while preserving legal punctuation and paragraph structure.
- **Content Digest:** SHA-256 checksum computed over the normalized document body.

---

## 2. Character-Bounded Passage Extraction

Rather than citing whole 100-page regulations, VERIDION's retrieval layer produces `EvidencePassageRecord` instances:

```json
{
  "passage_id": "pas_gdpr_art44_p1",
  "source_id": "src_gdpr_art44",
  "official_id": "32016R0679",
  "char_start": 12040,
  "char_end": 12450,
  "text_excerpt": "Any transfer of personal data which are undergoing processing or are intended for processing after transfer to a third country or to an international organisation shall take place only if, subject to the other provisions of this Regulation, the conditions laid down in this Chapter are complied with by the controller and processor...",
  "passage_digest": "9b1c28f...39a"
}
```

- **Passage Digest:** $\text{SHA256}(\text{text\_excerpt})$.
- **Verification Rule:** The independent verifier validates that $\text{text\_excerpt}$ exactly matches `document_text[char_start:char_end]`, ensuring offsets have not drifted due to document mutations.

---

## 3. Claim Citation Grounding & Support Assessments

Every claim generated by specialized agents (`REGULATORY_INTERPRETER`, `PRECEDENT_ANALYST`, `COMPLIANCE_STRATEGIST`, `SYNTHESIS_SPECIALIST`) must specify:
1. `passage_ids`: Array of referenced passage IDs.
2. `confidence`: Numerical support score $[0.0, 1.0]$.
3. `counterarguments`: Explicit objections and exceptions identified by the adversarial synthesizer.
4. `unresolved_questions`: Gaps in evidence requiring human judgment.

### Validation Rule
An analysis claim is marked `UNGROUNDED` if any cited passage ID does not exist in `evidence_passages` or if the passage text fails its content digest verification.

---

## 4. Mechanical Entailment vs. Legal Validity Disclaimer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Cryptographic and Mechanical Traceability ≠ Statutory Correctness**
> 
> Option C's passage verification guarantees that:
> 1. The cited passage was genuinely retrieved from the specified URL at the recorded timestamp.
> 2. The excerpt text has not been tampered with or corrupted.
> 3. The agent's reasoning was conditioned on that exact passage.
> 
> Option C does **NOT** guarantee that:
> 1. The statute has not been modified or superseded after retrieval.
> 2. The judicial precedent has not been narrowed or overturned.
> 3. The agent's interpretive inference constitutes valid legal advice under EU or member state law.
